Corporate Governance & Authority Rules

This branch defines who holds power, how decisions are executed, and where the lines between founders and investors are drawn. Board & Committee Structure: Composition of the Board of Directors, independent board members, and advisory committees. Reserved Matters & Voting Thresholds: A list of major actions that require supermajority investor approval. Thresholds of Authority (Delegation Matrix): Clear financial limits on what management can spend without board approval.

Capital Structure & Shareholder Regulations

This branch protects equity integrity and establishes rules for funding rounds, equity distribution, and shareholder changes. Share Class & Voting Rights: Definitions of Preferred vs. Common shares, voting weight, and dividend priority. Pre-emptive Rights & Anti-Dilution: Standard mechanisms allowing existing investors to maintain their ownership percentage in future funding rounds. Transfer Restrictions: Right of First Refusal (ROFR) — existing shareholders get the first right to buy shares if someone wants to exit. Drag-Along & Tag-Along Rights protect majorities during an acquisition offer and minority investors from being left behind. Vesting & Founder Lock-in: 3-to-4-year vesting schedules with a 1-year cliff for founders and key executives to guarantee long-term commitment.

Financial & Operational Boundaries

This branch sets financial discipline and defines how investment funds can and cannot be spent. Use of Proceeds (Budget Allocation): Strict rules ensuring funds raised are used specifically for agreed growth milestones (e.g., 50% equipment expansion, 30% marketing/logistics, 20% working capital). Debt & Leverage Caps: Limits on how much debt or third-party loans the company can take on relative to its equity or EBITDA. Treasury & FX Management: Guidelines for managing foreign exchange risk, cash holdings in local currency (RWF) vs. hard currency (USD), and bank counterparty limits. Financial Reporting & Audit Cadence: Mandatory schedules for monthly operational updates, quarterly financial statements, and annual audited financial statements by an accredited external firm.

Compliance, ESG & Operational Guardrails

This branch ensures legal integrity, operational resilience, and environmental/social responsibility. Regulatory & Tax Compliance: Mandatory compliance with local commercial regulations (e.g., RDB registration, RRA tax compliance, labor laws). Environmental & Sustainability Standards (ESG): Rules on chemical/detergent handling, wastewater treatment, energy consumption management, and eco-friendly practices. Data Privacy & IP Protection: Safeguarding operational software, customer data (such as WhatsApp transaction records), and proprietary workflows under applicable data protection laws.

Remedies, Default & Dispute Resolution

This branch details what happens when things go wrong, defining default scenarios and enforcement mechanisms. Events of Default: Explicit triggers for default (e.g., insolvency, material breach of contract, fraud, failure to provide audited financial statements for two consecutive periods). Remedies for Breach: Cure Periods (30-day window to rectify non-material breaches), Clawback Provisions (revocation of equity or bonuses from founders/executives in cases of fraud or gross negligence), and Investor Buyback Option (the right for investors to demand a buyback of their stake under severe default scenarios). Dispute Resolution Mechanism: Good-faith negotiation, then mediation, then binding arbitration.
